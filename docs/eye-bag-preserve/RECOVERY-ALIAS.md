## What changes, and what does not

The selected face's source-owned `eye_bag_preserve` recovery vector is shared by
documents that genuinely share that portrait graph and immutable source. A brush
changes only this effect's contribution through the existing recovery compositor.
It does not replace output pixels with the original photograph, erase another
portrait effect, modify ridge-preservation support, or copy donor vectors.

Every affected document retains its own colour, crop, repair stack and local
recipe. Unlike mixed-preset publication, recovery does not write a new document
recipe. The shared graph/support change and complete affected-set revisions have
one scoped ledger owner; ordinary single-document history must not record it a
second time. Immutable vector assets remain retained for undo and project replay.
A gesture with no canonical change is rejected before alias publication, with no
member advance or synthetic batch entry; viewing alone remains noncommittable.

## Uncertainty and lifetime

Prepared review authority is internal, not a client boolean. It may suppress the
dispatcher's current-document preadvance only after full revalidation; the scoped
transaction owns all actual member advances. Explicit preview cancellation removes
unpublished authority. Dispatcher housekeeping must not destroy an already prepared
review before its final verification.

An uncertain write is not cancellation or successful rollback. The original request
and proven batch identity remain available for diagnosis; no guessed receipt or
blind resubmission is allowed. Mutations remain fenced until a definite settlement
or the existing verified project-reopen boundary. Closing a panel or changing the
selected photo does not settle an unknown write.

## Technique and licence

The [GIMP layer-effects UI specification](https://developer.gimp.org/core/specifications/layer-effects-ui/)
describes a grayscale mask that modulates one effect independently. It is a design
reference only; it does not validate this renderer or prescribe shared-document
transaction semantics. The developer page is CC BY-SA 4.0. No code, text, weights
or assets were copied. This route reuses the repository's existing compositor,
immutable vector rasterization and pinned solver, without numerical changes.

## Explicit limits

- Only complete supported live shared-source member sets are admitted. Dormant,
  unavailable or stale inputs remain refused rather than skipped.
- The session review has a 600-second expiry. Individual display PNGs are capped
  at 4 MiB; the immutable engine image store has a 128 MiB reservation bound.
  Native delivery retains at most 16 MiB of image replies and evicts delivered
  bytes, keeping compact confirmation evidence. Paging avoids an all-images-inline
  transport limit, but does not establish measured large-batch performance.
- Ridge evidence remains manually reviewed, not automatic anatomical detection.
- The established opaque SDR and full-source decode/memory limits remain. Display
  PNGs are RGB8 reductions of full renders, not full-resolution output or HDR proof.
- Recovery coverage retains the existing uint8 precision. This does not introduce
  float-mask fidelity or change the one-pass common geometry sampler.
- Recovery vectors are not transferred across independent images or people.
- Native crop painting, cancellation of an in-flight generic recovery render, and
  other recovery families are not implicitly expanded by this alias route.
- All runtime, native/UI, history/reopen, numerical and photographic acceptance is
  UNRUN. No tests, builds, typechecks, product imports or inference ran.
