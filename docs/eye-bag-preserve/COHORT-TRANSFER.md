# Persistent eye-bag cohort review

Status: source development only. Every runtime, numerical, photographic, native,
UI, history, project-reopen and export acceptance case remains UNRUN.

This is a workflow extension of the existing manual-assisted `lnx.eye_bags.v2`
operation and persistent scalar association, not a new algorithm or automatic
ridge detector. Requirement `LNX-REF-PC-32464-C-e5fac5fdcf` remains partial.

## Ownership and scalar semantics

The existing version-1 association capsule remains scalar intent. Creating,
updating or unlinking an association does not migrate capsule versions, copy
donor geometry, copy protection/recovery vectors, or substitute another member's
analysis. Each independent document owns one explicitly reviewed face and its
actual effective original or derived source.

Explicit face overrides continue to take precedence over shared person intent.
A shared scalar change can therefore leave a member visually unchanged. The
workflow must show the actual fully resolved before/after result, not promise
that every member receives the source's appearance. Unlink removes the accepted
shared projection using the established semantics; it is not an implicit bake
of donor pixels or a reinterpretation of old recipes.

## Full source-plus-members review

The review covers the source first, then every requested member. It uses actual
complete mixed renders, including existing portrait effects, repair stacks and
target-specific support. Before and after each carry their own eye-bag support
scope, ridge digest and reviewed sides. Candidate neutrality cannot erase proof
of the active effect being removed, and a retained face override cannot be
mistaken for an inactive member.

Each member explicitly chooses whether an active candidate may replace its old
eye-bag correction. This choice cannot silently discard a selected nonzero
legacy effect. Neutral v2 intent does not itself clear legacy strength. Previously
retained positive legacy capsule intent may remain masked by an explicitly
approved member-local zero override; removing that override can reveal the old
intent again. It is not deleted, migrated or numerically reinterpreted.
Independently required portrait/collarbone evidence must remain present; one
family's ACK cannot replace another family's proof.

Actual before/after state and retained capsule content determine whether eye
review is mandatory. A generic update or unlink cannot evade review merely by
omitting eye-bag leaves from its new selection. The UI also offers explicit eye
review when a parked target's active effect is not known from source metadata.
Truly unrelated generic associations retain their established route.

## Persistence and uncertainty

The existing atomic association/graph ledger remains the only write and history
owner. The eye cohort retains exact membership and source-owned dependency
checks rather than reusing an ordinary scalar-only history marker for changes
to persons, links and capsules. Hidden aliases and dormant source histories
must not acquire an effect through an unreviewed create, patch, unlink or later
undo/redo.

Review images are immutable, bounded, lazily fetched session PNGs. They are not
durable source assets or history bitmaps. Every source/member image pair must
be loaded, checked and explicitly acknowledged. Unknown writes remain tied to
their original receipt and cannot be retried or declared canceled by dismissing
the review. Pre-handler refusal is distinct from a started transaction whose
outcome is uncertain.

## Limits and licensing continuity

Two to sixteen independent documents, including the source, are the bounded
cohort. Existing full-render memory/source/format limits remain authoritative.
Displays are RGB8 reductions up to a 1024-pixel edge, not native-resolution eye
inspection, source downconversion or HDR/alpha support.

An active before/after render needs its actual compatible solver and retained
support. The separate original neutral-reset access remains available; it does
not authorize bypassing review of an active cohort. Missing or changed target
evidence must be repaired/reviewed on that member, not borrowed from the source.

The numerical core, old recipes, licenses and primary-method research remain as
documented in `REQUIREMENT-AND-RESEARCH.md`. No third-party code, model, weights
or assets are introduced by this workflow. No quality, atomicity or performance
acceptance is inferred from source connectivity or authored fixtures.
