# Visible-support alias history contract

Source-authored / UNRUN. This slice adds no numerical algorithm, runtime,
photographic or native compilation acceptance claim.

## Distinct authority

The new `face_decoration_support_alias_review` marker is not the existing scalar
`face_decoration_alias_review`. It records the digest of the complete actual
image ACK and separately retains the original full-review digest. A publication
requires the selected face plus every before/candidate active decoration face,
all two-to-sixteen source-identical shared-owner documents, and the exact
original or immutable-derived source proof for each member. No mixed-preset,
scalar, effect-recovery, association or other-face mutation is permitted.

## Bank and pointer invariant

A proposal freezes the entire prior bank and candidate bank hashes. Publication
may append only the selected face's proposed immutable visibility record; it
cannot replace, remove or append unrelated records. Both pointer sides and their
exact immutable visibility records are persisted in the existing people ledger.
The dedicated decoration support validator resolves those records, never the
facial-recovery resolver.

The canonical bank remains append-only after successful commits. Undo restores
the exact old pointer, including absence for initial neutral painting, while
retaining both immutable records and unrelated history bytes. Redo restores the
exact new pointer. Frozen bank hashes describe the original proposal; they do
not authorize deleting later retained records or pretending undo recreates an
old whole-bank snapshot. Save/reopen requires the exact retained records.

## Atomicity and protected restore

Preparation renders each member's complete candidate with a detached staged
bank and that member's own crop, repair, colour and other recipe inputs. Before
publication and after expensive preparation, the original owner, graph, bank,
full member closure and dependencies must still match. Dormant or unavailable
affected members remain refused.

The existing people ledger publishes bank and graph together, advances every
member revision, and leaves document-only undo stacks alone. Compensation
restores graph and bank together only while identity/fences are still exact.
Monotonic fences are never rolled back. An uncertain intervening mutation is not
overwritten, and candidate bytes remain available for reconciliation. A known
ledger ID alone never reports successful publication or invents member results.

Protected undo/redo rechecks every member's original/derived source, exact face
analysis, source-owned supports and durable document dependencies. No member
recipe is copied or replayed. Typed support authority stays separate from scalar
authority; the old validator behavior is unchanged when the new marker is absent.

## Authored acceptance specifications

`engine/tests/test_face_decoration_support_alias_history_unrun.py` specifies
initial/revised records, full-member publication, detached preparation,
compensation/uncertain ownership, late receipts, save/reopen, immutable retention,
ACK binding, marker exclusion, changed crop/repair/colour/source and all-active
face coverage. These fixtures were not imported, collected or executed.
