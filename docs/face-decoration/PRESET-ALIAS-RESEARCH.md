The matching local architecture is the existing complete eye-bag mixed-preset
transaction, not the scalar-decoration or visibility-paint ledger. The selected
current document receives the entire chosen compatible recipe. Its siblings
share only the exact candidate portrait graph while retaining their own colour,
crop and repair state. Each member has its own original/derived provenance even
when the effective asset is shared. The target's reviewed visibility and recovery
assets remain immutable; a preset is not permission to paint or copy a mask.

Selected or retained-active decoration requires review. A colour/crop-only
preset can therefore have an unchanged portrait graph but a real current-state
change. No fake overrides or graph revisions are necessary. A total no-op is a
known no-write condition, not an invented history entry.

The private detached plan cannot execute through generic single-photo replay.
Only exact all-member image acknowledgment and a distinct composite authority
permit atomic graph/current-state publication, compensation and durable history.
Existing genuinely incompatible separate review owners remain explicit refusals;
selected effects must not be silently omitted to manufacture a successful preview.
