# Cohort and scalar-alias acceptance plan

All cases are **UNRUN**. Static checks do not establish runtime, history,
performance, native transport or photographic correctness.

## Persistent cohort

1. Create a confirmed persistent association with one source and distinct target
   owners. Transfer decoration pattern/strength. Every member must use its own
   measured face and independently reviewed visible skin, including the source.
2. Give each member different crop, colour, geometry, repair and retained face
   effects. Compare every displayed before/after with its canonical full-source
   render. No source-local settings or mask may silently replace target state.
3. Retain active decorations on enabled faces other than the linked person.
   Their source-owned support and resolved recipe must be included in evidence.
4. Update other selected association leaves while decoration remains in the
   capsule or any member's rendered graph. Full source-first decoration review
   must remain required, not depend solely on a selected-operation checkbox.
5. Unlink a cohort with active retained decoration and empty selected scope.
   Verify the exact operation semantics and all affected images; no unreviewed
   unlink, silent capsule migration or support copying.
6. Exercise admitted eye-bag, named-portrait and collarbone combinations. Shared
   image bytes must describe identical complete candidate states; each family's
   own coverage/consent remains required. Unsupported families refuse as a whole.
7. Change membership, owner, source, observation, visibility, repair or graph
   after image preparation. Old token and old ACK must refuse before publication.
8. Request any image with another family/token/member/revision/side/hash. No
   unrelated image or cached successful read may authorize this cohort.

## Shared-source direct scalar edits

1. Create two to sixteen available source-identical shared-owner documents with
   existing valid decoration support and different local photo settings. Edit
   pattern/strength through the dedicated alias review, not generic scalar patch.
2. Confirm every affected document's actual complete image pair. Before/after
   must preserve each document's crop, repair, non-portrait state and unrelated
   face effects. Only the proposed decoration leaves change in the shared graph.
3. Add an alias, hide/restore a source-bearing document, switch owner/source or
   change a dormant history while review is open. The frozen affected set must
   refuse if no longer complete. Independent copy/import owners stay independent.
4. Include enabled unmapped faces with active decorations. Their immutable
   visibility/recovery/source dependencies must be preserved in full images.
5. Verify neutral reset and identical scalar intent. Exact no-op must not invent
   a mutation revision or history entry. A real reset still shows active before
   pixels with valid original support rather than a fabricated neutral image.
6. Try to pass alias authority through user JSON, mutate another effect, replace
   support pointers or change association records. The private exact transition
   authority must reject all of these; it is not a general alias bypass.
7. Attempt initial/revised shared visibility paint. This scalar-only slice must
   retain a clear refusal, not mislabel scalar confirmation as support consent.

## Transaction and recovery

- Missing/reordered/duplicated acknowledgments, only one decoded image, image
  hash/size mismatch and late stale callbacks must never enable commit.
- Fail after preparation, before claim, during publication, after publication
  and before receipt delivery. Known failures and unknown writes must remain
  distinct; unknown writes are query-only and never blindly retried.
- Verify exact full-member undo/redo, project save/reopen, package/recovery and
  restored status classification. A changed source/support/repair or foreign
  graph must refuse restoration rather than overwrite unrelated edits.
- Verify immutable source PNG16/float master and canonical export remain
  unchanged by reduced RGB8 display transport. No review PNG belongs in history.
- Measure realistic two- and sixteen-member memory, latency and responsiveness
  separately; declared bounds are not measured performance or quality evidence.

Manual visibility, moderately frontal placement, opaque SDR, synchronous solve
and existing numerical budgets remain unchanged. This does not add automatic
skin/occlusion segmentation, proprietary material equivalence or new algorithms.
