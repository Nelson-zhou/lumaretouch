# Face-decoration preset and batch review acceptance

Every case below is **UNRUN**. Static syntax, source inspection and hash checks
do not establish renderer, UI, native receipt or photographic correctness.

## One-photo complete mixed preset

1. On an original opaque PNG16 portrait with independently reviewed visible skin,
   select a decoration material plus supported colour, geometry and crop edits.
   Compare before/after with independent canonical renders of the exact before
   and planned after snapshots. Check existing repair and unselected effects are
   present; a decoration-only overlay must not pass.
2. Repeat on an admitted opaque-SDR derived source with its independently owned
   analysis. Ensure no original-source pixels, masks or analysis leak into review,
   commit or export. Unsupported alpha/HDR refuses without conversion.
3. Retain active decoration on another enabled face while changing only another
   selected module. Require complete photographic review and include that other
   face's target-local support in the frozen dependencies.
4. Reset active decoration to neutral. Before must show the actual active result;
   after must preserve all unrelated effects. Missing/stale before support must
   not be represented by a fabricated neutral before image.
5. Exercise each original material and exact neutral. Verify recipe version and
   scalar values remain unchanged after review, commit, undo, redo, save and
   reopen. No donor source coordinates or support vectors enter a preset.
6. Change selection, load session, source, repair, crop, support, graph revision
   or document owner after preparing images. The old token/ACK must refuse.
7. Request before/after with wrong family, side, document, revision or PNG hash;
   corrupt PNG bytes or dimensions; decode only one side. None may enable Apply.
8. Compare a preset containing incompatible dedicated-review families. It must
   refuse clearly as a whole, not discard requested effects to obtain a preview.

## Ordinary explicit face-map batch

1. Use multiple distinct source sizes, skin tones, independent virtual-copy
   owners and supported original/derived sources. Map donor scalar intent to
   confirmed target FaceRefs. Target placement must be rebuilt from each face's
   own landmarks and visibility review; donor masks/recovery never transfer.
2. Inspect the actual full mixed image pair for every eligible target, including
   all target-local colour, crop, geometry and repair. Require each exact ACK;
   metadata-only, omitted, reordered, duplicated or foreign ACKs must refuse.
3. Include an enabled unmapped face with active decoration. Confirm before/after
   proof includes that face and its source-owned support, even when unchanged.
4. Include targets with selected neutral decoration, retained active decoration,
   no-op scalar transfer, unavailable native core, missing review and stale
   landmarks. Refusals must identify actual unmet dependencies, not invent pixels.
5. Review an ordinary 16-target batch through paged immutable PNG retrieval.
   Measure actual memory and latency separately. No inline aggregate transport
   workaround or silent detail reduction should change evidence quality.
6. Change target membership, source, repair, review support, revision or owner
   after preflight. Revalidate all targets before writes and reject stale proof.
7. Whole-state batch, shared-source aliases and persistent cohorts outside this
   contract must refuse active decoration, not borrow ordinary face-map consent.

## History, failure and uncertainty

- Inject failure before token claim, during image preparation, during commit,
  after graph publication and before receipt delivery. Only recognized exact
  before/candidate states may participate in rollback; foreign edits must remain.
- A read failure or cancellation must not become successful mutation or a photo
  ACK. A mutating unknown outcome stays query-only until exact receipt recovery.
- Close/reopen panels and repeat query after delayed callbacks, reload, expired
  image cache and interrupted decode. Stale image completions must not authorize
  a newer proposal. Re-preparation is required after lost evidence.
- Undo/redo and project/package/autosave reopen must preserve canonical recipe,
  source and support ownership with no secondary history owner or embedded
  display PNG snapshots. Export must use full source precision, not review RGB8.

## Existing numerical limits remain

The original Rust decoration kernel and materials are unchanged. Existing
moderately frontal/manual-visible-skin admission, 4M source ROI, 64 marks, 32M
triangle/pixel candidate budget and synchronous solve restrictions still apply.
Review images are display reductions, not native-resolution inspection or
automatic visibility detection. No vendor-equivalence or photo-quality claim.
