skin painting, effect-recovery painting or mixed-preset local-state changes.
These are source-owned dependency mutations, not pattern/strength leaves.

all live shared graph documents, prior support bank and proposed immutable
visibility asset. Render every document's complete candidate with that candidate
bank while retaining its own crop, colour, repair and other effects. Include all
active before/candidate faces and source/repair/geometry dependencies; an
initiating face thumbnail or metadata-only confirmation is insufficient.

pointer and the exact support-bank state through one atomic ledger. Rollback,
undo, redo, save/reopen and uncertain receipt reconciliation must restore both
parts together. A reused scalar authority must continue rejecting this operation.
Do not relax dormant/unavailable member closure without complete proof, silently
reuse another person's visibility mask, or copy donor recovery vectors.

Keep direct scalar support immutable in the existing protocol. Version or name
the support protocol separately so old frozen receipts retain their meaning.
