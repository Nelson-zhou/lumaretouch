### Evidence vocabulary and license boundary

“Source-connected” means a bounded implementation path exists in the cited
snapshot; it does not mean executed, visually accepted, release-ready or defect-free.
“Known incomplete” identifies an established missing path or defect. “In progress”
means no frozen/published completion claim. “No evidence in this bounded update”
means not assessed here, not proof of absence. “Missing” should only be applied to
an explicitly established absent requirement. All current records have unverified
runtime acceptance; no green CI, GPU, speed, installer or real-photo result is implied.
