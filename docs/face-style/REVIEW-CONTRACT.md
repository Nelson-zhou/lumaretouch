## Exact before/after and publication

New people actions are catalog, preview, image read and confirm, all bound to the
current enabled selected FaceRef and exact document/epoch/revisions. Reads require
an already active owner, never silently bind, migrate or reanalyse it. The normal
pending import/package/recovery/unknown-mutation guards run first.

Preview stages the real scalar patches against the complete inherited graph,
validates all effective recipe compatibility, and renders both complete current
source/repair/state graphs through the unchanged full-source renderer. It does
not apply then undo an effect on the live document. Original or opaque-SDR derived
sources keep exact effective content and original-file provenance distinct.
Unsupported analysis, invisible anatomy, neighbour/geometry constraints, alpha,
HDR or insufficient resources refuse; no proxy mask or cached effect is promoted
as full resolution.

Actual logical owner, active load session, FaceRef, full source/repair/state and
support/context, candidate graph, recipe and patch hash are frozen. Before/after
images are immutable separate pages, not metadata-only review. The front end
checks PNG bytes/hash/size and actual decode load before explicit acknowledgment.
Hashes of graph-only owner state and full graph-plus-context are distinct facts,
not incorrectly equated. Python proof hashes are not recomputed from a different
JavaScript/Rust float serializer.

Confirm rechecks current source/context and both images, then existing people
preparation runs. A final hook verifies the exact frozen candidate immediately
before canonical graph publication; post-publication verifies graph, source and
full export-snapshot parity before a success receipt or frame is delivered.
Canonical history owns one separate confirmation transaction. Unknown results
remain bound to the original full request and are query-only. No new history or
undo owner is introduced. Existing publication rollback remains authoritative;
post-publication disagreement cannot be reported as a safe no-write refusal.

## Explicit boundaries and acceptance still required

- The same project-owned source face may affect multiple identical-source logical
  documents. A single-photo review refuses this alias case; use an independent
  virtual copy or the established reviewed cross-image path, not unseen edits.
- Eight session reviews, 30-minute expiry, 4 MiB per PNG, 128 MiB/256-entry raw
  display storage. One page at a time fits the unchanged 8 MiB native result cap.
  Rendering still uses complete source/repair pixels. Display is RGB8 at maximum
  1024-pixel edge, not 100% precision inspection or measured performance.
- Invalid or changed source/support, manual markers, evidence/model availability,
  other edits, expired images and stale receipts require fresh review. No forced
  inferred anatomy, arbitrary fallback kernel or silent coefficient clipping.
- The qualitative names are local compositions, not proprietary pixel parity,
  automatic rejuvenation, a shape classifier or anatomical reconstruction.
- All fixtures, UI/native interaction, full-source numerical behavior, photo
  quality, identity/edge preservation, resource limits, save/load, undo/redo and
  preview/export acceptance remain UNRUN. Static checks do not establish them.
